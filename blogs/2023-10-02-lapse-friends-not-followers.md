In 2021 we launched the first version of Lapse , a social camera inspired by film photography. For the past year and a half we have been listening to our community’s feedback and evolving Lapse into what it is today: A camera for living in the moment and photo journal for close friends. We’re humbled that what we have built has resonated so strongly, with so many.
